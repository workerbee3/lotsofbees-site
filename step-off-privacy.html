Step Off Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what Step Off collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. Step Off is an iOS app made by Lots of Bees Inc., a company based in Canada ("we", "us"). It lets you track your steps and compete with friends in private step challenges called step-offs.

This policy covers the Step Off app only. Our other apps have their own policies.

Information we collect

We collect only what Step Off needs to count your steps, run leaderboards and step-offs, and measure our ads.

We do not use your device's location. City and country are only what you type in.

Health data

Step Off reads two things from Apple Health, only with your permission: your daily step count and your walking and running distance. It does not write anything to Apple Health.

  • We store one total per day (steps and distance) on our servers, so leaderboards, step-offs and your history work across devices. We do not store the underlying Health samples, times of day, or any other Health data.
  • Health data is used only to provide Step Off's features. It is never used for advertising, never shared with Tenjin or any ad network, and never sold.
  • You can stop Step Off reading Health data at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Step Off. Totals already uploaded stay until you delete your account.

How we use information

  • Run the app: show your progress and history, rank you on leaderboards, and total up step-offs.
  • Run your account: sign you in, send confirmation and password-reset emails, and delete your account when you ask.
  • Measure our ads: learn which ad campaigns led people to install Step Off, using the device and advertising data described below.
  • Keep things working: fix bugs and keep the service secure.

We do not sell personal information. We do not send marketing emails.

What other Step Off users can see

Step Off is social, so some of your information is visible to other people signed in to Step Off.

  • Leaderboards: your display name, city, country and your daily step and distance totals are visible to all signed-in Step Off users.
  • Step-offs: members of a step-off you join see your display name and your steps for each day of that step-off.
  • Not visible to others: your email address and your sign-in method.

Use a display name you're comfortable sharing, and leave city and neighbourhood blank if you'd rather not show them.

Advertising measurement and tracking

We advertise Step Off and use Tenjin to measure which ads lead to installs. Step Off itself shows no ads.

  • On first launch, iOS asks whether Step Off may track you. If you allow it, Tenjin receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you don't, the IDFA is never sent.
  • Either way, Tenjin receives the app's vendor identifier (IDFV), device model, iOS version, IP address and when you open the app. Tenjin may share attribution results with the ad networks we run campaigns on, and Apple's SKAdNetwork sends anonymous install reports to Tenjin.
  • What Tenjin does not get: your Health data, steps, name, email address or Step Off account.
  • Change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track for every app.

Tenjin handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Account → Delete Account.

Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes:

  • your account, profile and all your daily step and distance totals
  • your membership in every step-off
  • step-offs you created, for all of their members

Copies stay in our nightly database backups for up to 30 days, then are deleted. Device and advertising data held by Tenjin is kept under Tenjin's own policy, because Tenjin cannot link it to your Step Off account. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • See or change your profile any time in the Account screen.
  • Stop Health access in Settings → Health, and stop tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete your account in the app, or email us.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask a question about it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example Canada, the EU or UK, or California), you may have further rights, and you can complain to your local privacy regulator.

Children

Step Off is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Where your data is stored

We are based in Canada. Our database is hosted by Supabase in the United States (AWS us-west-2, Oregon). Our other providers may also process data in the United States or elsewhere, under their own security safeguards.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and our database only lets each signed-in user change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
